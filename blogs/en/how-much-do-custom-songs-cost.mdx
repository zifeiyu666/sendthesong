# How Much Do Custom Songs Cost in 2026?

**How much do custom songs cost?** For a personal gift in 2026, most buyers land between about **$20 and $265**, depending on whether the track is AI-generated or written by a named artist. AI custom songs usually sit in the **$20 to $50** range and arrive in minutes. Human-written songs usually start around **$179 to $265** and take days.

That spread is not a trick. You are comparing two different products: a preview-first personalized gift you can hear before paying, versus a commissioned performance you buy before it exists. This guide is for people shopping for a birthday, anniversary, wedding, memorial, or last-minute surprise who want a realistic number, not a vague "it depends."

## What actually moves the price

### Human performance versus AI production

This is the biggest split. A human service is paying a songwriter to write, perform, and produce a unique recording. That labor is why Songfinch and Songlorious sit near **$180+**. An AI service generates a personalized track from your names, memories, and style choices, which is why it can charge **$20 to $50** and play you a sample first.

Neither option is automatically "better." A $200 song is different: you are buying provenance and a human voice. A $30 song is different: you are buying speed, retries, and a lower-risk gift. Judge the lyrics and the replay value, not the sticker alone.

### Whether you can hear it before you pay

Traditional commissions usually require payment before the artist starts, because the work does not exist yet. Preview-first tools reverse that. On One Custom Song you can [create a free preview](/create-song), change lyrics or genre, and unlock the full track only when it sounds like the person you are gifting.

That workflow changes the real cost. A cheap song you cannot hear in advance can still feel expensive if it misses the story. A modestly priced song you can retry is often the better value.

### Length, rush fees, and revisions

Human services often price by length or artist tier. Rush delivery is a common upsell: extra **$60 to $100** is typical when a wedding date or birthday is already on the calendar. Revision policies are narrower than sales pages suggest. Some include one remake, some charge per lyric edit, some limit requests to 24–48 hours after delivery.

AI services usually treat speed as the default and let you regenerate samples before you pay. That is the main reason their advertised price is closer to the finished-gift price.

### Add-ons that inflate the total

The headline number is rarely the finished gift. Public add-ons in this category include extra verses, Spotify-style releases, vinyl, lyric PDFs, commercial licenses, and music videos. One Custom Song lists optional extras around **$7** for an MP3 download, **$7.99** for lyric wall art, and **$23.99** for a music video when those items are not already included in a plan. Promotions can make those add-ons free at checkout. Confirm the live numbers on [pricing](/pricing).

## How much does a custom wedding song cost?

Wedding and first-dance songs usually cost the same as any other custom song in the same production tier. The extra cost comes from timing. Fixed dates make rush fees more likely, and full-length dance tracks sit at the top of per-minute human packages.

If the wedding is more than a week away, a human commission can still work. If you need the song this week, an AI preview workflow is the cheaper way to avoid rush pricing. You can still play it at dinner, during a slideshow, or as a private gift the night before.

## Is a $200 custom song worth it?

It can be, if the point of the gift is "a real musician made this for you" and you have time to wait. It is a weaker deal if you mainly want personal lyrics, a shareable page, and the chance to fix a chorus that does not land.

A useful test: would you pay the advertised price without hearing a note? If the answer is no, use a preview-first service. If the answer is yes because the artist name matters, commission the artist.

## How not to overpay

1. Hear a sample of *your* song before you pay, not a demo of someone else's.
2. Ask whether the MP3 is included or sold separately.
3. Read the revision window, not the slogan.
4. Ignore crossed-out "was $99" anchors. Judge the number you pay today.
5. Order early enough that you do not need rush fees.
6. Count how many songs you will actually make this year before buying a plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much do custom songs cost?

Most personal custom songs cost **$20 to $50** from AI services and **$179 to $265+** from human songwriter marketplaces. One Custom Song currently starts at **$29.99** for a one-time song after a free preview. Check live [pricing](/pricing) for the number that will appear at checkout.

### How much does it cost to have a song written for you by a human artist?

Budget **$179 to $299** for a full-length personal track from services like Songfinch or Songlorious. Higher-listed artists can exceed **$399**. That price usually covers writing and recording, not rush delivery or extras.

### Can I get a custom song for free?

You can usually hear a preview for free. The finished downloadable song, share page, or gift extras are what you pay for. One Custom Song lets you [preview before unlocking](/create-song).

### Why do custom song prices vary so much?

The method of production. Human artists need briefing time, writing time, and recording time. AI tools generate a personalized draft in minutes. Add-ons, length, rush fees, and whether the MP3 is included then widen the gap.

### Is an AI custom song cheaper because it is lower quality?

It is cheaper because the production model is different. Quality depends on how specific your story is and whether you can revise before paying. A vague brief produces a generic song at any price.
